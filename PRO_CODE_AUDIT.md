## Engineering Laws (v0.4)

1. **Law of Autonomous Boundaries** — Every subsystem is autonomous. No cross-subsystem imports.
2. **Law of Contract First** — Define the interface before the implementation.
3. **Law of Graceful Degradation** — Every subsystem must handle its own failure.
4. **Law of Telemetry First** — If you can't measure it, you can't manage it.
5. **Law of Physics Fidelity** — Models must match physical reality. No hand-waving.
6. **Law of Scale Awareness** — Design for 200k GPUs, not 200.
7. **Law of Security by Default** — Zero-trust at every boundary.
8. **Law of Memory Hierarchy** — Hot → Warm → Cold → Frozen. Never lose data.
9. **Law of Fusion Modes** — Every operation must declare its fusion mode.
10. **Law of Circuit Breaking** — 3 strikes, then isolate. Countdown to recovery.
11. **Law of Atomic Operations** — Every tick is atomic. No partial state.
12. **Law of Documentation as Code** — Docs live with the code, not in a wiki.
